# Synthetic example: Harbor Components

This fictional company illustrates the format only. It has no ticker, no connection to a real issuer, and no price target.

| Area | Example observation | Confidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Business thesis | Replacement parts may create recurring demand. | Medium |
| Stock thesis | The market may already price in that durability. | Low |
| Catalyst | Next results could clarify inventory normalization. | Low |
| Risk | A customer concentration change could reverse the thesis. | Medium |
| Technical gate | Wait for a confirmed recovery structure before a hypothetical paper entry. | Low |

Falsification: a sustained decline in replacement demand combined with margin compression would invalidate the starting thesis.
