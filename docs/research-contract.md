# Manual research contract

1. State the question and time horizon before gathering evidence.
2. Preserve a source link, publication date, and a short claim for each important fact.
3. Keep business quality, valuation, catalysts, risks, and technical setup as separate dimensions.
4. Label estimates as estimates; do not present them as facts.
5. Write a falsification condition and a review date.
6. Treat any output as educational analysis, not a buy, sell, or hold instruction.

The workflow is intentionally manual: it encourages source checking and clear reasoning instead of opaque automation.
